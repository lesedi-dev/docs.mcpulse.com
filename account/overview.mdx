**Settings** in the sidebar opens five pages. `/settings` on its own redirects to the first.

| Page | Contents |
|---|---|
| [My account](/account/profile) | Your full name, your email, and deleting the account |
| [Company](/company/profile) | Account name, website, phone, city, state, country, timezone |
| [Team](/team/manage) | Members, roles and invitations, in one table |
| [Notifications](/notifications/preferences) | Event × channel matrix |
| [Billing](/billing/plans) | Usage against plan, and the plan cards |

Entering settings swaps the sidebar for its own nav, opening with a link back — no section is somewhere you need the browser's back button to leave.

## What is deliberately not here

**Theme.** It lives in the account menu — in the sidebar on desktop, behind the avatar on mobile — because it is a preference you flip, not a form you fill in.

**Passwords.** Sign-in is a magic link, so there is no password to change.

**API keys.** They belong to an MCP, not to the account. A key belongs to one server, and the place you want it is the MCP you just created. See [Create a key](/api-keys/create).

## One account per person

MCPulse settles you into exactly one account. If someone invited your address, you join theirs with the role they gave you; otherwise you get a new account and become its owner. This happens on your first account-scoped request, not on your first write. See [Authentication](/authentication).
